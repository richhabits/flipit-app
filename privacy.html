Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026. Publisher: Hectic Radio Ltd.

In short: FLIP IT collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer.

What the app does with data

  • Your practice-run data is read from the folder or address you choose, and shown on your device. It is kept on your device (and in the app's shared storage so the Watch and widgets can show it). It is never uploaded by the app.
  • Folder access uses Apple's Files picker. The app can only read the folder you pick, and only the files it needs.
  • An optional local server address and token (for advanced users) are stored on your device; the token is kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Notifications are created on your device. No push service is used.
  • Explanations use Apple's on-device intelligence where available. Only the warning text is used, and it is processed on your device.
  • The built-in sample is bundled with the app. Using it sends nothing anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not connect to any broker or place orders.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and loads nothing from third parties.

Third parties

If you install through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. We receive no personal information from Apple beyond what Apple provides to all developers.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, open an issue at our support page. For UK users, the data controller for this website is Hectic Radio Ltd; you may also contact the ICO.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is published here.
